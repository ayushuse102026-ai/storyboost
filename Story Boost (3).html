How to use this app (4 easy steps)
  1. Fill the form below and tap Build creator brief. Send it to a creator.
  2. When they send a draft, paste it in Story check.
  3. Fix anything marked ✗, then check again.
  4. Use Budget to see how much money you need, and Creators to keep track of who you asked.

Words: a creator is a person who posts videos or photos online. A boost means paying so more people see their post.
